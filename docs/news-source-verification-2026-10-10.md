## Scope and limitations

- ILO retail: conclusions were adopted at the meeting held 28 September–2 October 2026. Publication on 8 October is distinct from the meeting dates. The story supports agreement on priorities, not achieved implementation or measured safety improvements.
- ILO labour mobility: reports discussion and institutional support for cooperation at the second Doha Dialogue in Tanzania. It does not establish that qualification recognition, wage protection or portability of benefits has been implemented across participating states.
- Eurostat: published 1 October, reporting August 2026 seasonally adjusted unemployment. Rates were 6.4% for the euro area and 6.1% for the EU; EU youth unemployment was 15.4%. These are statistical estimates, not recruitment vacancies or a forecast.
- Valencia: 14 charging points were reported opened. Expansion to 50 in 2027 and investment of up to EUR 40 million are plans. The EUR 40 million refers to PragmaCharge’s Spanish programme, not necessarily the cost of this individual hub.
- Renault: EUR 600 million covers an announced 2026–2030 programme, including five models and a battery assembly line. More than 6,000 jobs means existing direct jobs the programme intends to support, not newly created positions. The reported total includes company funds and public funding.
- Florentia Village: opening, EUR 250 million project investment and 190 stores are reported by Türkiye’s investment-promotion agency. Subsequent expansion and more than 5,000 direct jobs are targets, not verified realised effects. No independent audit of investment or job figures was undertaken.
